Technical Implications

Implementing the identified gaps requires specific upgrades to the existing stack (Socket.io, Tensorflow, Firebase).

  • ➜
    For Video Calling:

    Upgrade Socket.io to handle WebRTC signaling or integrate a service like Agora/Twilio. Peer-to-peer data usage concerns.

  • ➜
    For Panic Button:

    Requires background geolocation permissions and SMS gateway integration (Twilio/Africa's Talking) for emergency contacts.

  • ➜
    For Dynamic Pricing:

    Complex backend logic needed. Will strain the current matching engine. Needs caching layers (Redis) for real-time price updates.

Recommendation Summary

1. Immediate Priority: Safety

Implement SOS/Panic Button and Insurance Coverage info. Trust is the currency of the gig economy.

2. Operational Fix: Cancellations

Build a Cancellation Policy Engine. Without strict rules, revenue leakage and user frustration will occur.

3. Growth: Marketing Tools

Add Promo Codes & Vouchers. Referrals are good, but direct acquisition campaigns need coupon logic.
